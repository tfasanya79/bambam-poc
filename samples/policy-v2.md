# Remote Work and Data Access Policy

## 1. Purpose

This Policy explains how employees may work remotely, and how they access company systems while off-site. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

## 2. Approved locations

Employees should work from a company-approved location. Approved locations are the home office, a company office, or a client site registered with IT, subject to manager approval.

## 3. Password and authentication

All employees must rotate their account passwords every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for every system that stores customer data.

## 4. Device requirements

Laptops used for remote work must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

| Control | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Disk encryption | Mandatory |
| Screen lock | 5 minutes |
| Backup | Daily |

## 5. Reporting a lost device

An employee must report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 48 hours of discovery.

## 6. Data residency

Customer data must remain in the European Union region. Copying customer data to a system outside the EU is prohibited.

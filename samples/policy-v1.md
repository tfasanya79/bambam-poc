# Remote Work and Data Access Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees may work remotely and how they access company systems while off-site. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

## 2. Approved locations

Employees must work from a company-approved location. Approved locations are the home office, a company office, or a client site registered with IT.

## 3. Password and authentication

All employees must rotate their account passwords every 60 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for every system that stores customer data.

## 4. Device requirements

Laptops used for remote work must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic screen lock after 10 minutes of inactivity.

| Control | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Disk encryption | Mandatory |
| Screen lock | 10 minutes |
| Backup | Weekly |

## 5. Reporting a lost device

An employee must report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours of discovery.
